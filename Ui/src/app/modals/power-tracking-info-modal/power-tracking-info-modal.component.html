About Power Tracking & Management

This page tracks the combat power of your alliance members over time.

Power Tab

The table shows each player's most recent record: their 3 squads (with power and type — Tank, Air, or Missile), Total Hero Power (THP), and Kills.

  • Growth history icon: view how a player's power has developed over time.
  • Edit icon: load all recorded entries for a player to correct or delete them.
  • New Power Record: add a new record for a player (top right).
  • Export: download the current list as Excel or CSV to import into your own spreadsheet tool.
Access Tokens Tab

Generate a shareable link so players can submit their own records or view their own growth history without logging in. Each token can optionally be given an expiration date, and can be revoked at any time or replaced with a new one.
